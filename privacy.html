Privacy Policy — gogcli

Last updated: 19 September 2026

gogcli is a command-line tool operated by a single individual for personal use with their own Google accounts. It runs locally on the operator's own computer.

What is accessed

When authorized, the tool accesses Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Contacts and Tasks data belonging to the account that granted consent.

What is stored

OAuth refresh tokens are stored locally in the operating system keychain. No Google user data is transmitted to, stored on, or processed by any remote server.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which invalidates the stored tokens immediately.
